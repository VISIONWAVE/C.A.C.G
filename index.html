Who We Are

A family built on grace, growing in purpose.

Since our founding in 2018, Christ Alone Christian Group and Prophetic Ministry (C.A.C.G.) has been a spiritual home for individuals and families in Ilaro, Ogun State — offering sound biblical teaching, fervent prayer, and warm community.

Led by Prophet Olusegun Oladeji (H.O.J. Idunnu Jesu), we exist to help people encounter God, discover their purpose, and live it out with courage.

Through discipleship, prayer, fellowship, mentorship, and practical Christian living, we raise a generation of purpose-driven, Christ-centered, kingdom-minded youths and young adults who influence their campuses and communities for Christ. We are a mission field and training ground for destiny, equipping young people to live out their faith boldly, excel in their calling, and become agents of godly transformation wherever God has placed them.

Our mission is to raise extraordinary youths and young adults who are passionately on fire for God, rooted in His Word, and empowered by the Holy Spirit — helping them discover their God-given purpose, develop their gifts, and walk confidently in His will. Our desire is to see every young person know God, be fire-branded, and become a light that points others to Christ.
