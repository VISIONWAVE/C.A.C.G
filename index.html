Who We Are

Christ Alone Christian Group and Prophetic Ministry (C.A.C.G. Int'l) is a Spirit-filled ministry committed to raising people, building capacity, and advancing the Kingdom of God through the Word of God and the power of the Holy Spirit.

We create spaces for sound teaching, fervent prayer, prophetic encounters, worship, discipleship and practical Christian growth.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of believers who are rooted in God's Word, empowered by the Holy Spirit, equipped for purpose, and prepared to impact their world with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
